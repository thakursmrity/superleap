The problem

Sales leaders can see who is underperforming, but not why, and coaching happens in weekly reviews long after the moment has passed. AI ROI gets reported as hours saved, which doesn't help a CXO justify renewal.

Who it's for

  • Reps in their first year, who get in-the-moment guidance
  • Team leads, who see which behaviours separate their reps
  • CXOs, who get one metric that shows AI is changing outcomes

How it works

  • Learns behaviour patterns from calls, messages and stage changes of top-quartile reps
  • Spots the same moments in other reps' pipelines and suggests the move, with its reason
  • The rep acts or skips with a reason; skips improve the model
  • A weekly performer gap index on the leadership dashboard

What we'd measure

  • Performer gap index, week over week
  • Nudge acceptance rate, and conversion on accepted nudges
  • Time to productivity for new reps
  • Renewal and expansion rate of accounts using Coach

First version

Three high-signal patterns per industry, built on call summaries and activity data Superleap already captures, with nudges inside the lead view and the gap index on one dashboard tile.

Risks and open questions

  • Reps may read nudges as surveillance; framing as "what top reps do" matters
  • Small teams may lack enough data per pattern; pooled industry benchmarks could fill the gap

Why I'm close to this

At Xorosoft I helped drive 80% CSAT, a 22% drop in repeat tickets and a 4.9 out of 5 G2 rating across 94 reviews, by finding the patterns behind good outcomes and making them repeatable. Coach applies the same idea to sales reps.
