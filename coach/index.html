Product vision

1.Problem

Leaders can see who is behind, but not why. Coaching happens in weekly reviews, long after the moment has passed. The few moves that make top reps great live in their heads, not in the CRM. And AI ROI is reported as hours saved, which does not help a CXO justify renewal.

2.Users and jobs

Team leadCoach a large team with limited timeThe few moves worth teaching this week, and which ones are spreading

3.How it works

  1. LearnFind behaviours that separate closed leads from lost ones, using call summaries, messages and stage changes.
  2. SpotWatch for the same moment in every rep's pipeline: a brochure reopened, two missed calls, a fee question.
  3. SuggestShow the move inside the lead view, with the evidence behind it and the time window.
  4. Learn againThe rep acts or says not now with a reason. Both teach the model, and the gap index updates weekly.

4.Design principles

The point is to lift people, so the product has to feel like a helpful senior colleague, not a scoreboard.

1

Compare moves, not people

The reason always describes the lead's behaviour and what works, never another rep.

Not: "Your top 5 reps do this."
2

You vs you

A rep sees progress against their own last four weeks. No leaderboards in the rep view.

Not: a team ranking.
3

Credit the source

When a move spreads, the rep it came from is named and thanked. Top reps become teachers.

Not: anonymous "best practice".
4

The rep can say not now

Every move can be skipped with a reason. Reps know their leads, and the model learns from them.

Not: a mandatory task.
5

Everyone gets moves

Moves trigger on lead behaviour, so new and top reps both get them when the moment comes.

Not: only for low performers.
6

The gap is a team number

Leadership sees the gap across the team. Individual move data stays with the rep and their direct lead.

Not: a tool for review-day evidence.

6.First version

In scope

  • Three high-signal moves per industry, starting with education admissions
  • Built on call summaries and activity data Superleap already captures
  • Moves shown inside the lead view, with reason and time window
  • Act or not now with a reason
  • Team lead view of moves and adoption, with credit to the source rep
  • Gap index on one leadership dashboard tile

Out of scope for v1

  • Live in-call prompts (Coach SuperAgent covers the call itself)
  • Auto-actions without the rep, such as sending messages
  • Individual rankings or leaderboards
  • Moves learned across customers without consent
  • Tying moves to incentives or compensation

7.Risks and mitigations

RiskLikelihoodMitigation
Reps read moves as surveillanceMediumMoves-not-people copy, you vs you progress, no rankings, sentiment as a guardrail metric
Managers use move data to shame repsMediumGap index only at team level; individual data visible to the rep and direct lead only
A move is correlation, not causeHighCompare accepted moves against similar leads without one; retire moves that stop lifting conversion
Small teams lack data per moveMediumStart with pooled industry patterns, with customer consent, then personalise as data grows
Too many nudges become noiseMediumCap at a few moves a day, only above a confidence threshold

8.Roadmap

Now (v1)

Three moves, one number

Prove reps act on moves and that accepted moves lift conversion in one industry.

Next (v2)

Moves learned per team

Each customer's own playbook emerges from its data; team leads can add and retire moves.

Later (v3)

Moves that act

With rep approval, SuperAgents draft the WhatsApp or schedule the call, closing the loop from moment to action.
