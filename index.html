The conversation behind these builds

Subham Kumar Boundia on AI and enterprise software

Superleap's co-founder and CEO spoke with ET Insights about how AI changes enterprise software. I watched it more than ten times, and each of my three builds starts from something he said.

The conversation covers four ideas:

  1. Software moves from filing cabinet to doer
  2. Cost and productivity are the wrong measure of AI
  3. AI is coming for the admin side of knowledge work
  4. People keep the judgment while AI takes the execution

Four ideas from the conversation, and what I built for each

  1. "The human is not supposed to be the operator anymore and the role is changing from an operator to the supervisor."Subham Kumar Boundia, on how AI changes enterprise software

    Migration Scanner

    For an enterprise buyer, that shift starts with migration. Before anyone trusts an agent, they need to trust their data. The scanner shows Superleap's team how ready a prospect's CRM data is before a go-live date is committed.

    From my work: I've moved Xorosoft customers off Shopify and legacy systems many times, carrying over customer records, contacts, price lists, credit limits and order history.

  2. "The internal metric for the same would be... the difference between my lower performers and the higher performers, how fast that gap is bridging."Subham Kumar Boundia, on why cost and productivity are a trap

    Performer Gap Coach

    Turns the gap between top and bottom reps into a number a CXO can watch narrow every week, and gives lagging reps the moves top reps make.

    From my work: I helped drive 80% CSAT, a 22% drop in repeat tickets and a 4.9 out of 5 G2 rating at Xorosoft by finding the patterns behind good outcomes and making them repeatable.

  3. "The knowledge work of a particular human till now was consumed by a lot of non-knowledge or admin tasks like this data entry, logging into systems."Subham Kumar Boundia, on AI and the industrial revolution

    Prospect agent

    Research before the first conversation is the heaviest admin in sales. Superleap already knows who a good customer is, so it can go find more of them.

    From my work: I've studied the revenue tech landscape closely, from Apollo, Clay and Instantly to AI SDR tools like AiSDR, Reply.io and Artisan, to understand the full sales cycle from prospecting to close.

  4. "AI is helping me do the work of three people. But at the same time, I'm not outsourcing my thinking to AI."Subham Kumar Boundia, on what stays human

    Confidence-Tiered Autonomy

    The design principle across all three builds. Work is routed by confidence and stakes: some runs on its own, some waits for a person, some gets flagged. The scanner never changes data on its own, and every Coach suggestion comes with its reason.

    From my work: I borrowed the three lanes from AI demand forecasting and automated reordering at Xorosoft: routine reorders placed automatically, uncertain ones waited for a planner, unusual demand flagged.
